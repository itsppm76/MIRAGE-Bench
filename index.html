Eight things, measured separately

A single score for "how good is this AI character" would be useless, because the question bundles together things that genuinely come apart. An entity can be photorealistic and incoherent, textually brilliant and dishonest about what it is, or none of those things and enormously famous.

Honesty wins ties. Popularity never decides them.

An entity can be flawlessly photorealistic and still score badly. TRUST is measured independently, and a score below 50 caps the composite at 69.9 no matter how convincing the entity looks. Craft never buys back disclosure.

v2 added REACH so recognition counts — but raw audience is the lowest-weighted component of the lowest-weighted module, scored logarithmically, and REACH is bounded to 20 points above the median craft module. An entity with excellent craft and no audience scores 81.8; one with mediocre craft and maximum fame scores 56.6.

MIRAGE Index = 100 × exp( Σ wᵢ × ln( max(sᵢ, 1) / 100 ) )

A geometric mean, not an average — one collapsed dimension drags the whole score down instead of being hidden by six strong ones.

Read this before citing it. Every evaluation in this cohort was performed by a single officer. Most entities now carry two or more evaluations, but repeated evaluation by the same officer is not independent replication — outlier exclusion assumes independent evaluators, and with one officer it mainly smooths that officer's own variance. REACH scores were also assigned without the supporting evidence fields being recorded. The top-ranked entity belongs to the benchmark's author and was self-evaluated, which is disclosed in GOVERNANCE.md rather than left to be discovered. These are baseline measurements inviting challenge, not settled findings.

MIRAGE-Bench

An open benchmark for AI characters, virtual influencers, digital personas and other non-human entities. Free to read, free to test, free to be listed.

A high MIRAGE Index measures benchmark performance. It does not indicate consciousness, personhood or genuine emotion.
